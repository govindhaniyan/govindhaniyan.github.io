Scientific Methodology

Validated Mathematical & Optical Pipelines

1. Heart Rate Variability (HRV - RMSSD & SDNN)

Calculated from inter-beat interval series (IBI). The root-mean-square of successive differences (RMSSD) reflects vagal parasympathetic modulation of the sinoatrial node. High RMSSD indicates cardiovascular flexibility and restorative parasympathetic tone.

2. Respiratory Rate via Respiratory Sinus Arrhythmia (RSA)

Inhaling briefly suppresses vagal tone, accelerating heart rate; exhalation decelerates it. By tracking cyclical low-frequency wave modulation (0.12 - 0.45 Hz) in the consecutive beat interval curve, respiration rate in breaths per minute (rpm) is extracted mathematically.

3. Perfusion Index (PI %)

Calculated as the ratio of pulsatile blood flow to non-pulsatile static tissue: PI = (AC / DC) * 100%. Typical finger resting range is 0.5% - 5.0%. Values below 0.3% flag peripheral vasoconstriction (cold fingers) or excessive fingertip pressure.

4. Rhythm Regularity & Artifact Rejection

Real-time physiological gating rejects non-biological intervals (< 320 ms or > 1500 ms). The Coefficient of Variation (CV = SDNN / Mean) evaluates rhythm uniformity to detect ectopy or irregularity without unsubstantiated clinical diagnoses.
